# Privacy Policy

## 1. Who is responsible

Simple Training is a free workout planner and logbook. It is currently a personal project operated by **Fovae, a project of Vladyslav N.** (“Fovae”, “we”, “us” or “our”). If the project becomes a registered business or offers a paid version, we will update this information.

Contact: [fovae.work@gmail.com](mailto:fovae.work@gmail.com)

Simple Training is currently available at <https://fovae.github.io/training/>. If we move it to a custom domain, we will update this Policy and the app’s legal-document links.

Fovae is responsible for personal information it receives, such as messages sent to the contact email. The app is designed so that your profile, plan, workout history and notes stay in your browser on your device and are not sent to Fovae.

## 2. At a glance

- You do not need an account and we do not ask you to sign in.
- The app does not include advertising.
- The app does not currently use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies.
- Your profile, training plan, workout records and notes are stored locally in your browser. We do not receive or have access to them.
- GitHub Pages hosts the app. When your device requests the site, GitHub may process technical request and security information, such as your IP address. We do not have access to GitHub’s hosting logs.
- The app creates backup files in readable JSON format. It does not encrypt them or upload them to Fovae. You choose where to save or share them.

## 3. Who may use the app

Simple Training is intended for people aged 16 or older. The app asks you to confirm that you are at least 16; it does not ask for or store your date of birth for this check. The optional age field in your profile is for your own use and is stored locally.

Please do not use the app if you are under 16. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 16 has contacted us or otherwise provided personal information to us, contact us at the email address above. We will take reasonable steps to address the information we hold. We cannot inspect or delete data stored only on that child’s device.

## 4. Information stored on your device

The app stores information in browser storage on your device, including IndexedDB, local storage and cache storage. This is needed for the app to work, remember your settings and work offline. The app may also use a service worker to provide offline access and deliver app updates.

Depending on the features you use, locally stored information can include:

- required body weight, height and experience level;
- optional name or nickname, age and sex selection;
- language, theme, units, timer and other settings;
- training plans, exercises, dates, sets, weights and repetitions;
- workout and exercise notes you enter; and
- which versions of the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use you accepted, and when you accepted them.

This information is processed on your device for the app’s functions, such as showing your plan, recording workouts, displaying progress and applying any rule-based training hints. It is not sent to Fovae. The app does not use it to create an account, advertise to you or build a profile about you on our systems.

You control what you enter. Notes are free text and could contain sensitive or health-related information. Please avoid entering information you do not want stored in your browser.

Body measurements, sex selection, workout records or notes may be personal data and, depending on their content and context, could reveal information about health. The app does not ask for a diagnosis or medical history and does not send these local records to us.

## 5. Information we may receive

### Messages you send us

If you email us, we receive the information in your message and the contact details associated with it. We use it to respond and handle the matter you raised. Please do not email us workout records, health details or backup files unless necessary for your request.

Our contact email uses Google Gmail. Google may process email information on servers outside the country where you live, including outside the European Economic Area. Please review [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [information about Google’s data transfers](https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks).

### Technical information handled by GitHub Pages

The app is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub. When you visit the app, your browser must make a network request to GitHub’s servers. GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address, request details, timestamps and security logs to deliver and protect the hosting service. Fovae does not operate GitHub’s servers and cannot see or retrieve those logs.

GitHub handles this information under its own privacy information and terms. Please review [GitHub’s Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement). We do not control GitHub’s precise log retention, its use of service providers, or any international transfers it makes. We do not direct or access these logs, so we do not describe GitHub as our processor for them.

## 6. Analytics

Analytics are **not currently enabled**. The app does not currently send page-view or installed-app launch analytics.

We may add minimal, cookie-free analytics in a future version to understand general app usage. If enabled, the planned measurements may include page views, an estimate of unique visitors, approximate country, device type and a one-time signal that the installed app was launched for the first time. They will not include profile information, body measurements, age, sex, training plans, workout records or notes.

The analytics provider has not yet been selected. Before enabling analytics, we will update this Policy to identify the provider, explain the data it receives, its legal role, retention and any relevant international transfers, and provide any choice or notice required by law. Until that update is published, analytics remain off.

## 7. Backups and restore

The app creates a backup file containing your app data in readable JSON format. The app does not encrypt the file or upload it to a Fovae server. You choose where to save or share it, for example through your device’s file manager or share sheet. If you send or save the file using email, cloud storage or another service, that service receives the file and handles it under its own terms and privacy information. Anyone who obtains an unprotected copy may be able to read its contents.

Keep the file somewhere secure, share it only with people and services you trust, and delete extra copies when you no longer need them. Restore validates the file before importing it, but validation does not protect a backup from someone who obtains or reads it.

## 8. Cookies and similar technologies

The app does not currently use cookies for advertising, tracking or analytics. It uses browser storage and caching technologies, including IndexedDB, local storage, Cache Storage and a service worker, to provide features you request, remember settings and support offline use. These are used for the app’s operation, not to track you across other sites.

You can remove locally stored app data using **Settings → Delete all data**. Deleting the app icon from your home screen does **not** delete data stored in your browser. To remove that data, use **Settings → Delete all data** or clear the site data in your browser settings.

If we add analytics or other non-essential storage in a future release, we will update this Policy and provide any consent or control required by law before that technology is used.

## 9. Legal bases and data protection rights

Your profile, plan, workout history, notes and document-acceptance records are stored and used locally by the app on your device. Fovae does not receive or access these records. This Policy does not claim that Fovae is the controller of that local processing.

For personal information in emails we receive, our legal basis is our legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR in responding to messages, handling requests and keeping a proportionate record of correspondence. Our interest is to communicate with people who contact the project. We consider this use limited to that purpose and proportionate to the information involved.

You may have rights under data protection law, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, and in some circumstances data portability. These rights apply to personal information for which Fovae is responsible; they do not give us access to information that remains only in your browser. You can view, change or delete local app information yourself using the app’s settings or your browser’s site-data controls.

If you contact us with a data request, we will look for personal information that we actually hold, such as your email correspondence. We cannot retrieve or erase local workout or profile records because we do not receive them.

You may complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission (DPC), the supervisory authority in Ireland. Information is available at [dataprotection.ie](https://www.dataprotection.ie/).

## 10. Retention and security

Local app information remains in your browser storage until you delete it, clear the site’s browser storage or the browser removes it. Fovae has no copy and cannot restore it if it is lost. We retain email correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, follow up and keep appropriate records.

The app is served over HTTPS and validates imported backup files before use. No internet-connected service or local device can be guaranteed completely secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and any backup file you create.
